Social Automation

Social Automation is a content tool for small businesses and the agencies that look after them. The business owner (or their agency) drops in their own photos and video, the tool prepares captions and a posting plan, and nothing is published until a person approves it. Approved posts are then published to the business's own accounts on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Threads and YouTube.

What the TikTok integration does

  • Lets the account owner connect their own TikTok account with TikTok Login.
  • Uploads videos that the account owner has created and approved, either as a draft in their TikTok inbox or as a post.
  • Never posts without approval, never reads messages, never interacts with other accounts.
