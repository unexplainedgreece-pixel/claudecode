Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Social Automation ("the tool") publishes posts to social media accounts on behalf of their owners. This policy explains what data the tool handles and how.

1. Data we receive from platforms

When you connect a TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, Threads or YouTube account, the platform gives the tool: a user identifier, your display name, and access tokens that allow posting on your behalf. For TikTok this means the user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish permissions. The tool does not receive your password, your followers, your messages or your viewing history.

2. Where the data lives

The tool runs on the operator's own computer or server. Access tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256) in the tool's local database. They are never sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them.

3. Content

Photos, videos and captions you provide are stored locally and, when a platform requires a public link, on the operator's Cloudflare R2 storage. They are used only to publish the posts you approve. Published metrics (views, likes, comments counts) are fetched to show you a performance report.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data goes only to the social platforms you connect, when you publish.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when you click Disconnect in the tool or revoke the app in the platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Content and metrics are deleted when you delete the post in the tool. You may request deletion of everything by email.
